About Lara

HR Leader. Executive Coach. Organisational Consultant.

I bring 15 years of senior HR leadership experience across pharmaceuticals, FMCG and retail in different Middle East markets, combining strategic HR and commercial experience with a strong focus on leadership development, people and performance.

My experience with organisations including JTI, Philip Morris International, AstraZeneca, Merck and MAF Retail has shaped my understanding of how to connect business strategy with practical HR action.

My MS in Executive Coaching & Organisational Consulting from NYU, together with my ICF coaching education, has deepened my understanding of leadership, human behaviour and organisational development, complementing my practical HR and business experience.

I connect strategy, people and leadership to create meaningful performance and impact.

My approach

Executive & Career Coaching

My coaching is empathetic, authentic and evidence-informed, grounded in the ICF Code of Ethics and Core Competencies. I create a confidential, non-judgmental space where clients can reflect, challenge assumptions and explore new perspectives.

Strengthening leadership effectiveness through clarity and purposeful action.
