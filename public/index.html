About Lara

HR Leader. Executive Coach. Organisational Consultant.

I bring 15 years of senior HR leadership experience across pharmaceuticals, FMCG and retail in different Middle East markets, combining strategic HR and commercial experience with a strong focus on leadership development, people and performance.

My experience with organisations including JTI, Philip Morris International, AstraZeneca, Merck and MAF Retail has shaped my understanding of how to connect business strategy with practical HR action.

My MS- Executive Coaching and Organisational Consulting -NYU ECOC, together with my ICF ACC credential, has deepened my understanding of leadership, human behaviour and organisational development, complementing my practical HR and business experience.

I connect strategy, people and leadership to create meaningful performance and impact.
